# Citrus vocabulary

A long-form note on citrus species and their culinary names.

Pomelo is the largest citrus fruit and an ancestor of the grapefruit.
Kumquat, unusually, has a sweet peel and sour flesh.

Buddha's hand is a fingered citron with no pulp.
